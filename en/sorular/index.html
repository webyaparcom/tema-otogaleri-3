Glossary

Inspection, claims record, insurance: the terms

Tap a term to highlight its questions. For information only; check official sources for current rules.

Inspection2Claims record2Insurance1Replaced / painted1Notary1
Inspection01

What does an inspection report show?

The results of body (painted, replaced parts), engine, gearbox, underbody and electronics checks; the date and the issuing firm are on it too.

Inspection02

Can I have the car inspected where I choose?

Yes; we take the car with you to the inspection firm of your choice by appointment. Whoever orders it pays the fee.

Claims record03

What is a claims (Tramer) record?

The record of damage payments reported by insurers for the car; it is checked with the chassis number.

Claims record04

Does a claims record mean the car was in a crash?

The amount alone does not tell how serious the damage was; read it together with the inspection report.

Insurance05

Comprehensive vs compulsory traffic insurance?

Traffic insurance is compulsory and covers the other party’s loss; comprehensive cover is optional and protects your own car.

Replaced / painted06

What does “locally painted” mean?

Only part of the panel was painted; “painted” means the whole panel, “replaced” means a new panel was fitted.

Notary07

Where and how is the sale made?

The transfer is done at a notary; payment can also go through the notary’s secure payment system. The dealer books the appointment.
